Per-line alignment — contenteditable prototype

Each line is its own element with its own text-align. Click a line (or drag across several), then align — only the touched lines change, and the caret stays glued to the text on centred/right lines.

Try: caret on line 2 → Align centre (only line 2 moves). Drag from mid-line 1 to mid-line 3 → Align right (all three move — “every touched line”). Type on a centred line — the caret follows the text. Enter at the end of a centred line — the new line inherits the alignment. Alignment travels with its line as you edit.
